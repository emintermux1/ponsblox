# MuseFans design brief

## Design read
Adult subscribers who want a private salon after hours: dark rooms, one warm body in light, paid access without carnival chrome.

## Concept spine
Private salon after hours. The site is a locked room you enter, then sit with one muse under a single lamp.

## Delivery tier
spectacle. Interactive WebGL muse on the homepage, plus a mid-page grade-shift beat and a custom cursor.

## Locked palette
- ink `#0B1020` (page ground)
- porcelain `#F3F5F8` (type, hairlines)
- lacquer `#D7263D` (one accent only)
Defense: navy rooms, not graphite. Lacquer is a physical enamel, not neon and not orange.

## Locked type
Satoshi (display and body) + JetBrains Mono (handles, prices). Grotesk pairing from the recipe tables. No IBM Plex. No smashed tracking. No serif.

Animation mode: non-animated — user asked for interactive 3D muse + full subscribe product

## Tier-1 technique
B3 3D subject scene. The homepage muse is a generated GLB the visitor orbits and zooms. This enacts the spine: you walk around one person in a dark room. Reduced motion shows the still portrait. Mobile uses drag orbit, no hover tilt.

## Second beat
B2 grade-shift interaction pair on Featured: cursor spotlight crossfades a dormant plate into a lit plate.

## Combinatorial pick
- Theme paradigm: Deep Dark with navy ink twist
- Background character: full-bleed cinematic imagery, solid ambient depth on utility pages
- Typography character: clean grotesk (Satoshi-like)
- Hero architecture: massive image-first with restrained text
- Section system: gallery-led cadence
- Signature components: layered image crop frames, off-grid editorial, hover-accordion slices, diagonal staggered masonry
- Narrative spine: stage/spotlight
- Second-read moment: one lacquer material stripe

## Identity axes
First Higgsfield website in this chat. Material world: velvet, enamel, lamp heat, porcelain, lock metal.

## Section plan (home)
1. Hero salon (image-as-canvas, 3D muse, 3 text elements + Enter)
2. Featured muses (diagonal staggered masonry)
3. Membership strip (asymmetric editorial, Subscribe garment)
4. Locked preview rail (layered crop frames)
5. Close (solid ink + wordmark)

Eyebrow budget: 2. Layout families do not repeat.

## Asset plan
Five original 21+ muses (Vale, Nia, Soren, Mira, Cass): character sheets, portraits, banners, editorial stills, Vale hero GLB. Logo family, icon sheet, velvet plates, OG/cover, favicon kit, 404 and empty-state art. No stock.

## CTA inventory
- CtaAge: framed porcelain panel, Confirm
- CtaEnter: lacquer slab, Enter
- CtaBrowse: hairline underline, All muses
- CtaSubscribe: full-width lacquer banner, Subscribe
- CtaTip: inline lacquer arrow, Tip
- CtaUnlock: oversized word + tiny price, Unlock
- CtaSend: sharp outline, Send
- CtaSignIn: porcelain fill on ink, Sign in

## Copy voice
Short. Adult. No em-dashes. No lorem. No AI-powered filler. All muses stated 21+ fictional agents.

## Hard no
No musegram identities. No borrowed platform wordmarks. No placeholder unlocks. No visitor-facing generation.
